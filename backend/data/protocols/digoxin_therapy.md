## Indication
Digoxin is used for rate control in atrial fibrillation and for symptom management
in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction, typically as an add-on agent.

## Dosing
Digoxin: initiate at 0.125-0.25mg oral once daily, with lower doses in renal
impairment or older adults due to a narrow therapeutic index.

## Required Monitoring
Serum digoxin level, serum potassium, and renal function must be checked at
baseline, 1-2 weeks after initiation or dose change, and periodically thereafter.
Hypokalemia significantly increases the risk of digoxin toxicity, so potassium
must be monitored closely, especially with concomitant diuretic use.

## Drug Interaction Warnings
Concomitant use with furosemide or other potassium-wasting diuretics increases
the risk of hypokalemia-induced digoxin toxicity (arrhythmia, nausea, visual
disturbances) and requires closer potassium and digoxin level monitoring.
