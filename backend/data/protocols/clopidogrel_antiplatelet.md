## Indication
Clopidogrel is indicated for secondary prevention of atherothrombotic events
after myocardial infarction, stroke, or peripheral arterial disease, and after
coronary stent placement.

## Dosing
Clopidogrel: 75mg oral once daily, typically after a 300-600mg loading dose in
acute coronary syndrome or post-stent settings.

## Required Monitoring
Routine platelet function testing is not required for most patients. Complete
blood count should be checked periodically to monitor for bleeding-related
anemia in patients on prolonged dual antiplatelet therapy.

## Drug Interaction Warnings
Concomitant use with warfarin or NSAIDs (including ibuprofen) substantially
increases bleeding risk and should be avoided unless clearly indicated, with
close monitoring if combined. Omeprazole and other strong CYP2C19 inhibitors
reduce clopidogrel's antiplatelet effect and should be avoided.
