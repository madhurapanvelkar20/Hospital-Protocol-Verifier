## Indication
Losartan, an angiotensin receptor blocker (ARB), is indicated for hypertension,
diabetic nephropathy, and in patients who cannot tolerate ACE inhibitors due to
cough or angioedema.

## Dosing
Losartan: initiate at 50mg oral once daily, up to a maximum of 100mg once daily
based on blood pressure response.

## Required Monitoring
Serum creatinine and serum potassium must be checked at baseline and within
1-2 weeks of initiation or dose titration, due to risk of hyperkalemia and
renal function changes, similar to ACE inhibitors.

## Contraindications and Warnings
Losartan is contraindicated in pregnancy due to risk of fetal harm. Avoid
combination with ACE inhibitors or potassium-sparing diuretics without close
potassium monitoring, due to additive hyperkalemia risk.
