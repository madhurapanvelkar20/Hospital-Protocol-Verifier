## Indication
Warfarin is indicated for prevention of thromboembolism in atrial fibrillation, venous
thromboembolism treatment, and mechanical heart valve prophylaxis.

## Dosing
Warfarin: initiate at 5mg oral once daily, adjusted based on INR response and patient
factors (age, liver function, concomitant medications).

## Required Monitoring
INR must be checked at baseline, every 2-3 days during initiation, then weekly until
stable, and at least monthly once therapeutic range is achieved. Target INR range is
typically 2.0-3.0 for most indications.

## Drug Interaction Warnings
Concomitant use of NSAIDs (including ibuprofen) with warfarin significantly increases
bleeding risk due to combined antiplatelet and anticoagulant effects and should
generally be avoided; if unavoidable, more frequent INR monitoring is required.
Amoxicillin and other antibiotics can potentiate warfarin's anticoagulant effect by
altering gut flora and vitamin K metabolism, and INR should be checked within 3-5 days
of starting concomitant antibiotic therapy.
