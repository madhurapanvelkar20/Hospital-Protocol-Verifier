## Indication
Methotrexate is first-line disease-modifying therapy for moderate-to-severe rheumatoid
arthritis.

## Dosing
Methotrexate: initiate at 7.5-10mg oral once weekly, with folic acid supplementation
1mg daily on non-methotrexate days to reduce toxicity.

## Required Monitoring
Complete blood count (CBC) and liver function tests (LFT) must be checked at baseline,
every 2-4 weeks for the first 3 months, then every 8-12 weeks thereafter, due to risk
of myelosuppression and hepatotoxicity.

## Drug Interaction Warnings
Concomitant NSAID use (including ibuprofen) increases the risk of methotrexate
toxicity by reducing renal clearance of methotrexate, and should be used cautiously
with increased monitoring of CBC and renal function if combined.
