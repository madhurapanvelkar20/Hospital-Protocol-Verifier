## Indication
Telmisartan, an angiotensin receptor blocker (ARB), is indicated for hypertension
and for cardiovascular risk reduction in patients unable to tolerate ACE inhibitors.

## Dosing
Telmisartan: initiate at 40mg oral once daily, up to a maximum of 80mg once daily
based on blood pressure response.

## Required Monitoring
Serum creatinine and serum potassium must be checked at baseline and within
1-2 weeks of initiation or dose titration, due to risk of hyperkalemia and
renal function changes, similar to other renin-angiotensin system agents.

## Contraindications and Warnings
Telmisartan is contraindicated in pregnancy due to risk of fetal harm. Avoid
combination with ACE inhibitors or other ARBs without close potassium monitoring,
due to additive hyperkalemia risk.
