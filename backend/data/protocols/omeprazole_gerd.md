## Indication
Omeprazole, a proton pump inhibitor, is indicated for gastroesophageal reflux
disease, peptic ulcer disease, and prevention of NSAID-induced ulcers.

## Dosing
Omeprazole: initiate at 20mg oral once daily before breakfast, up to 40mg once
daily for erosive esophagitis, for a duration guided by symptom response.

## Required Monitoring
Routine lab monitoring is not required for short-term use. For long-term use
(over 1 year), periodic monitoring of magnesium and vitamin B12 is recommended
due to risk of hypomagnesemia and B12 deficiency with chronic acid suppression.

## Drug Interaction Warnings
Omeprazole significantly reduces the antiplatelet effectiveness of clopidogrel
by inhibiting its hepatic activation (CYP2C19), and concomitant use should be
avoided when possible; consider an alternative acid suppressant such as
famotidine if a PPI cannot be avoided.
