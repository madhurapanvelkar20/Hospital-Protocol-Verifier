## Indication
Levothyroxine is first-line replacement therapy for primary hypothyroidism.

## Dosing
Levothyroxine: initiate at 1.6mcg/kg oral once daily on an empty stomach, with
lower starting doses (25-50mcg) in older adults or those with cardiac disease.

## Required Monitoring
TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone) must be checked at baseline and every 6-8
weeks after initiation or dose change until stable, then annually. Free T4 may
also be checked in patients with pituitary disease.

## Contraindications and Warnings
Use with caution in patients with untreated adrenal insufficiency or acute
coronary syndrome. Absorption is reduced by calcium, iron, and proton pump
inhibitors; separate dosing by at least 4 hours when co-prescribed.
