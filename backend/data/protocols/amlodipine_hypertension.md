## Indication
Amlodipine, a dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker, is indicated for
hypertension and chronic stable angina.

## Dosing
Amlodipine: initiate at 5mg oral once daily, up to a maximum of 10mg once
daily based on blood pressure response.

## Required Monitoring
Routine laboratory monitoring is not required. Blood pressure and heart rate
should be reassessed at each visit, and patients should be monitored for
peripheral edema, a common dose-related side effect.

## Contraindications and Warnings
Use with caution in patients with severe aortic stenosis or decompensated
heart failure. Combination with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors can increase
amlodipine levels and the risk of hypotension and edema.
