## Indication
Furosemide is indicated for management of fluid overload symptoms in heart failure.

## Dosing
Furosemide: initiate at 20-40mg oral once or twice daily, titrated to symptom control
and daily weight.

## Required Monitoring
Serum electrolytes, particularly potassium and sodium, along with renal function
(serum creatinine), must be checked at baseline, within 1-2 weeks of initiation or
dose change, and periodically thereafter, due to risk of hypokalemia and
hyponatremia.

## Contraindications and Warnings
Use with caution in patients with severe hypokalemia or hyponatremia until corrected.
Monitor closely when combined with ACE inhibitors or potassium-sparing diuretics.
