## Indication
ACE inhibitors such as Lisinopril are first-line therapy for stage 1-2 hypertension,
particularly in patients with diabetes or chronic kidney disease.

## Dosing
Lisinopril: initiate at 10mg oral once daily. Titrate every 2-4 weeks based on blood
pressure response, up to a maximum of 40mg once daily.

## Required Monitoring
Serum creatinine and serum potassium must be checked at baseline, within 1-2 weeks of
initiation, and at every dose titration, due to risk of hyperkalemia and acute kidney
injury. Blood pressure should be reassessed at every visit.

## Contraindications and Warnings
ACE inhibitors are contraindicated in pregnancy and in patients with a history of
angioedema. Use with caution in patients with bilateral renal artery stenosis. Avoid
combination with potassium-sparing diuretics without close potassium monitoring.
