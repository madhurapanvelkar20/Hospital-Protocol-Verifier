## Indication
Insulin is indicated for type 1 diabetes, and for type 2 diabetes when oral
agents are insufficient or contraindicated, or during acute illness/hospitalization.

## Dosing
Insulin: dosing is individualized based on weight, glycemic targets, and
concurrent therapy; typical starting basal dose is 0.1-0.2 units/kg/day,
titrated based on blood glucose monitoring.

## Required Monitoring
Blood glucose must be monitored at least before meals and at bedtime during
initiation and dose titration. HbA1c should be checked every 3 months until
stable. Patients and caregivers should be educated on hypoglycemia recognition
and management.

## Contraindications and Warnings
Use with caution in patients with renal or hepatic impairment due to altered
insulin clearance and increased hypoglycemia risk. Dose adjustments are often
required during acute illness, surgery, or changes in oral intake.
