## Indication
Prednisone is used for a wide range of inflammatory and autoimmune conditions,
including rheumatoid arthritis flares, asthma exacerbations, and inflammatory
bowel disease.

## Dosing
Prednisone: dosing is highly indication-specific, ranging from low-dose
(5-10mg oral once daily) for chronic maintenance to high-dose (40-60mg once
daily) for acute flares, tapered gradually to avoid adrenal insufficiency.

## Required Monitoring
Blood glucose and blood pressure should be monitored periodically during
therapy, particularly in patients with diabetes or hypertension. Bone density
(DEXA scan) should be considered for long-term use (over 3 months).

## Drug Interaction Warnings
Concomitant NSAID use (including ibuprofen) increases the risk of
gastrointestinal ulceration and bleeding, and should be used cautiously with
consideration of gastroprotection (e.g., a proton pump inhibitor) if combined.
