## Indication
Atorvastatin is indicated for primary and secondary prevention of atherosclerotic
cardiovascular disease in patients with elevated LDL cholesterol, and is commonly
co-prescribed in patients with type 2 diabetes given elevated cardiovascular risk.

## Dosing
Atorvastatin: initiate at 10-20mg oral once daily, up to a maximum of 80mg once
daily based on lipid response and cardiovascular risk.

## Required Monitoring
Liver function tests (LFT) should be checked at baseline. Routine periodic LFT
monitoring is not required in the absence of symptoms, but should be repeated if
signs of hepatotoxicity develop. Creatine kinase should be checked if the patient
reports muscle pain or weakness.

## Contraindications and Warnings
Avoid combination with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors at higher doses due to increased
myopathy and rhabdomyolysis risk. Use with caution in active liver disease.
