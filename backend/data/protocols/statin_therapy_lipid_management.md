## Indication
Simvastatin and other statins are indicated for primary and secondary prevention of
atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease in patients with elevated LDL cholesterol.

## Dosing
Simvastatin: initiate at 20mg oral once daily in the evening, up to a maximum of 40mg
once daily due to increased myopathy risk at higher doses.

## Required Monitoring
Liver function tests (LFT) should be checked at baseline. Routine periodic LFT
monitoring is not required in the absence of symptoms, but should be repeated if signs
of hepatotoxicity develop. Creatine kinase should be checked if the patient reports
muscle pain or weakness.

## Contraindications and Warnings
Avoid combination with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors at doses above 20mg due to increased
myopathy and rhabdomyolysis risk.
