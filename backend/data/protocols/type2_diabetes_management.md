## Indication
Metformin is first-line pharmacologic therapy for type 2 diabetes mellitus in patients
with adequate renal function.

## Dosing
Metformin: initiate at 500mg oral twice daily with meals. Titrate gradually up to a
maximum of 2000mg per day based on glycemic control and tolerability.

## Required Monitoring
Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) or serum creatinine must be checked at
baseline and at least annually thereafter, or every 3-6 months in patients with
reduced renal function. HbA1c should be checked every 3 months until stable, then
every 6 months.

## Contraindications and Warnings
Metformin is contraindicated in patients with eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73m2 and should
be used with caution (reduced dose) between 30-45 mL/min/1.73m2. Metformin should be
temporarily discontinued in patients with acute kidney injury or before procedures
using iodinated contrast media, due to risk of lactic acidosis.
